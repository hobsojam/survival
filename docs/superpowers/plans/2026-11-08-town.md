# The Town Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Slice 1 of the town: after the survivor makes the hall home, a lost traveller arrives, asks for work, takes a job, and the survivor builds them the first house.

**Architecture:** New game modules hold the town: `town.ts` for state and its rules, `jobs.ts` for what workers do at dawn, `traveller.ts` for the arrival and the ask, and `townSites.ts` for house plots and their building. They follow the castle's patterns: pure rules called from `applyAction` and `advanceTime`, prompts and taps that agree, and panels laid out from tested views and tested DOM helpers. Townsfolk positions are a pure function of the clock, drawn by `sceneVisuals`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Phaser, Vite, Vitest, oxlint.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-town-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No new dependencies and no image files. Sprites are drawn in code.
- Copy is plain and warm, not poetic, with no exclamation marks. Townsfolk are never given gendered pronouns in copy; use their name.
- Townsfolk have their own names, and the player never renames them. Nothing in the town can be lost for good, fail, or need upkeep.
- Everything is reachable by tap as well as by key, and works when the survivor is weak.
- SonarCloud needs 80% coverage on new code, and `App.ts` and the scene classes are untested. Any DOM or scene logic goes in a tested helper.
- `SAVE_VERSION` goes from 39 to 40 in this slice. It must be the only open PR changing the saved shape.
- The town is an `EASTER EGG:` feature. Mark new code with `EASTER EGG: the town, see src/game/town.ts`, add the town to the list in `AGENTS.md`, and keep it out of the README.
- Every task ends green on `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run lint` and the task's tests. The last task also runs `npx vitest run` and `npm run build`.

## Review Focus

1. **The survivor isn't on the hills when the traveller comes.** The arrival happens at nightfall wherever the survivor is, and the ask is answered from the Camp tab, so it never depends on being at the hall. *(Task 3 tests it.)*
2. **Three refusals.** The traveller leaves, and a new one with the next name comes 10 days later, so the town can't be lost for good. *(Task 3 tests it.)*
3. **A full larder.** A worker's eggs or honey that won't fit stay in the coop or hive for another day. They are never lost. *(Task 2 tests it.)*
4. **Saves.** A version 39 save whose hall is already home upgrades with an empty town, and sees the traveller at the next nightfall. A save reloaded mid-ask still asks. *(Tasks 1 and 3 test these.)*
5. **Moving back to the house after the town has started.** The town carries on: the ask, the job and the house all still work. Only the first arrival needs the hall to be home. *(Task 3 tests it.)*

**Files:**

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `TownState`, `HouseState` and `HOUSE_NEEDS` from Task 1.
- Produces:
  - `HOUSE_PLOTS: readonly { area: AreaId; x: number; y: number }[] = [{ area: 'hills', x: 31, y: 23 }]`
  - `houseStage(house: HouseState): 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4`. Stage 1 is footings, at 10 stone or more. Stage 2 is walls, at 30 stone. Stage 3 is the roof, at 4 beams. Stage 4 is the door and hearth, at 1 fitting.
  - `townSpots(state: GameState): { plot: number; area: AreaId; x: number; y: number }[]`. A plot is open once someone is waiting for a house (a person with `house: null`) and the house isn't finished.

Each go lays one stone, one beam or one fitting, in that order, paid from the bag and the cart for stone (as `spendCastleStone` does) and from `castle.goods` for beams and fittings. It costs the castle's work time and energy. The stage lines are `'The footings are in.'`, `'The walls are up.'`, `'The roof is on.'` and `'You hang the door and lay the hearth. The house is finished.'`. Other goes say `'You lay another stone on the house.'` or `'You fit another beam.'`.

These are the castle's remaining need plus the town's unbuilt beams or fittings, minus what's already made. Hangings stay castle-only. The goods rows' "enough" copy says `'There is enough ${good} for now.'` once both are met.

## Later slices

Each later slice gets its own detailed plan when it starts, written from what slice 1 taught. In order:

1. **Word gets around.** More plots on the hills (8 to 12 in all), and a newcomer arriving some days after each house is finished. Newcomers take the remaining camp jobs: bakery loaves, the wood store, the beds, the field and the mill, the sheep, and the orchard. Add more greetings.
2. **The square.** Its paving, a well and the stalls, built a piece at a time. Once the camp jobs run out, newcomers keep the square and run the stalls.
3. **New ground.** Something to build to reach the slope beyond the hills, such as a bridge, then more plots there.
4. **Market day and the hook.** The square opens once every house is full and every stall has someone. Workers' surplus goes to the stalls. On the first market day, traders arrive, and one cart carries the glass lens. Add the journal lines, and the town is finished.
