# Orchard Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Eating or cooking an apple saves its pips, which the player plants on new ground at camp and, years later, picks apples from.

**Architecture:** A new `src/game/orchard.ts` holds the saved shape and every rule, including growth worked out from each tree's planted day and the calendar. `rules.ts` gains two small handlers, and `eat` and `cook` save pips. The scene draws the plot and trees through `visuals.ts`, and reaches them through `pickTapTarget` and `prompts.ts`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Phaser 3, Vite, Vitest, oxlint. Art is drawn in code.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-orchard-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No new dependencies and no image files.
- Copy is plain, warm and short, with no exclamation marks. The orchard goes in the README.
- `SAVE_VERSION` becomes 36, with an upgrade step at key 35. This must be the only open PR changing the save format. Never edit `tests/fixtures/save-v23.json`.
- SonarCloud needs 80% coverage on new code and `App.ts` is untested, so logic stays in tested helpers.
- Commit messages end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. A pip planted on the last day of summer, or the first of autumn: the tree bears at its third autumn, no sooner, and not before day-count arithmetic says a whole number of autumns have passed.
2. Eating or cooking an apple with pips plus trees already at 6, or after the first pip: no pip beyond the cap, and "You keep the pips." appears only once.
3. A version 35 save upgrades to no pips, no orchard and no trees, and the morning note doesn't fire for it, or again in a later year.
4. A tap on a tree that has nothing to offer walks there and does nothing; it doesn't refuse, stick, or take a neighbouring tree's action.
5. Standing where two spots are both in reach: the prompt and E act on the nearest, and picking one tree never changes another's ripe day.

### Task 2: Pips, planting, growing and picking

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/game/orchard.ts`, `src/game/rules.ts:59,767,798,1206,1267`, `src/game/news.ts`, `src/game/journalEntries.ts`, `src/ui/yearJournal.ts:28`, `src/art/journalIcons.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1's shape.
- Produces, from `orchard.ts`:

  - `TreeStage = 'sprout' | 'sapling' | 'young' | 'grown'`, and `treeStage(tree: OrchardTree, day: number): TreeStage`, for 0, 1, 2, and 3 or more autumns passed

  - `treeRipe(tree: OrchardTree, day: number): boolean`: bears, and `ripeDay <= day`
  - `nearestSpot(state: GameState): number | null`: the closest spot in `BALANCE.interactionRadius`, once the orchard stands, at camp
  - `plantBlocker(state: GameState, spot: number): string | null` and `pickTreeBlocker(state: GameState, spot: number): string | null`
  - `spotAction(state: GameState, spot: number): GameAction | null`: `plantPip` for an empty spot if `plantBlocker` is null, `pickOrchardApple` for a ripe tree, otherwise null
  - `savePips(state: GameState, apples: number): { state: GameState; note: string }`
  - `orchardNews(before: GameState, after: GameState): string[]`
- Produces, in `GameAction`: `{ type: 'plantPip'; spot: number }` and `{ type: 'pickOrchardApple'; spot: number }`.

  Also test, each as one `it`:
  - **Pips from eating:** eating an apple adds one pip and says `'Ate an apple. You keep the pips.'`; a second adds one more with the message `'Ate an apple.'`.
  - **Pips from cooking:** `bakedApple` and `applePie` each add one, and the first time says the note. Other dishes and other food add none.
  - **Rotten apples:** an apple lost to spoiling adds none.
  - **The cap:** with pips plus trees at 6, eating an apple leaves pips unchanged and gives the plain message.
  - **Planting:** `plantPip` spends a pip, fills the spot with `{ plantedDay: day, ripeDay: day }`, and costs `gatherEnergyCost` and `gatherSeconds`. Refusals: `'Stand beside the tree.'`, `'This tree is too young for apples.'`, `'The tree bears apples in autumn.'`, `'The tree has no ripe apples yet.'`, `'Make room in your bag for an apple.'`.

  - **Morning note:** `orchardNews` gives `'There are apples on your own tree this morning.'` when the oldest tree goes from 2 to 3 autumns, and nothing the autumn after, nor for a younger tree reaching 3 later.
  - **Journal:** the first pick each autumn writes `'Picked an apple from a tree I grew myself.'` on the autumn page; a refused pick writes nothing. The line is off the page until the orchard stands.
