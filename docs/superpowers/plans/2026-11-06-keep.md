# The Stone Keep Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Let the player raise one square stone tower at the top of the hills path, stone by stone, then go in through its door to sleep at night or climb to the top by day.

**Architecture:** A new `src/game/keep.ts` owns the keep's numbers, its blockers and `inKeep(state)`, a check worked out from the state alone. The existing warmth, wolf, alarm, sickness and pony rules each ask `inKeep`, so nothing about the night needs a saved "sleeping here" flag. The scene draws the tower from a frame name worked out from the state, and a small tested `doorFade.ts` plays the fade.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Phaser 3, Vite, Vitest, oxlint; art drawn in code.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-keep-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No new dependencies and no image files.
- `SAVE_VERSION` **38** with an upgrade step, and it must be the only open PR changing the save format. Never edit `tests/fixtures/save-v23.json`.
- 60 stone in all: footings to 8, ground floor to 24, door at 24, upper walls to 48, top to 60. Door: iron hinges and 4 wood. Iron hinges: 2 ore, 1 wood, made once.
- `isAtHome` stays about the house or campfire. The keep adds no cooking, rest spot or stores.
- Copy is plain and warm, not poetic, with no exclamation marks.
- Keep logic in tested helpers: SonarCloud needs 80% on new code and `App.ts` is untested. Don't add a literal to App's `{ type }` dispatch union if it would exceed 25; hinges go through the existing `data-upgrade` route.
- Everything works by tap as well as by key, and every path works when the survivor is weak.
- Each task ends with `npx vitest run` green. The last also runs `npm test`, `npm run lint`, `npm run build` and the browser path.
- Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. **A night in the keep with the camp fire out.** On a winter night with a ration in the bag and no fire at camp, the survivor keeps the ration, stays warm and isn't woken by the bird (Task 2).
2. **Night falling on the walk to the door.** A door tapped by day and reached after nightfall must sleep, not climb, and a door tapped before dark and reached in daylight must climb (Tasks 2 and 3).
3. **The wood for the door when the bag has none.** With wood only in the handcart or the store at camp, hanging the door works from the hills and takes the wood from there (Task 1).
4. **The pony at night.** Unfinished keep: it goes home at nightfall as now. Finished keep: it stays while the survivor is on the hills, except in winter. Leaving the hills at night drops its spaces. An over-limit bag still saves in every case (Task 2).
5. **The fade.** Input during a fade does nothing, a pause or reload during one can't leave the survivor hidden or the camera moved, and it always ends (Task 3).
