# The Hills Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A new area past the quarry with ore and a wild pony, a forge added to the workshop that makes horseshoes and an iron pickaxe, and a shod pony that walks beside the survivor carrying extra.

**Architecture:** Game rules live in a new `src/game/hills.ts` (area, ore) and `src/game/pony.ts` (trust, carrying, where it is), reached through `applyAction` handlers in `rules.ts`. The forge and its two items are three more `UpgradeId`s in `workshop.ts`. The pony is drawn from a pure `ponyPoseFor` and eased by `AmbientLife`, like the animal friends, but is not a `Friend`, so it gives no gifts or keepsakes.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Phaser 3, Vite, Vitest, oxlint. Art in code.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-hills-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No new dependencies and no image files.
- `SAVE_VERSION` becomes 37, in one upgrade step. Never edit `tests/fixtures/save-v23.json`.
- Copy is plain and warm, not poetic, and has no exclamation marks. Use the copy in this plan as written.
- No crops, gathered food, new rabbit art or rabbit behaviour, riding, stables, pony feed, gifts or keepsakes from the pony.
- The pony can't die, sicken, leave or lose trust. Nothing is ever taken from the bag.
- Keep logic out of `App.ts`, which is untested. SonarCloud needs 80% on new code.
- Commit messages end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. **A bag over its limit because the pony isn't there.** `saveGame` silently refuses an invalid state, so a validator that uses the current capacity would stop the game saving without a word. Saves must stay valid (Task 4).
2. **Both ways of changing area.** `move` and `cross` each change `state.area`. Finding the hills, the pony reaching home and the hooves and snow lines must work through both, and the quarry now has two exits (Tasks 1 and 4).
3. **Tapping the pony on a phone.** The walk must stop in offering range without startling it, wherever it has moved to (Task 6).
4. **Heavy ore with nowhere to go.** Three ore fill six of eight spaces long before a forge can exist. Dropping must work and the ore must be recoverable (Task 2).
5. **Old saves.** A save from any version, including one standing in the quarry or holding stone piles, loads with no hills, no ore and its piles intact (Task 1).
