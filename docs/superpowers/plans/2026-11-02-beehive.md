# Beehive Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A straw beehive on the north ground that makes one honey every three dawns in every season, holds up to two, and gives them up from a Beehive panel.

**Architecture:** This follows the bakery's pattern.
- `src/game/beehive.ts` holds the dawn step, the blocker and the take.
- `rules.ts` wires up the dawn, the build and the `takeHoney` action.
- `panels.ts` has a tested view, and `App.ts` renders it.
- Art lives in `phase3Sprites.ts` and `visuals.ts`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Phaser, Vitest, oxlint (`--deny-warnings`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-beehive-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Honey all year: one honey every **3** dawns, in every season, whether the survivor is at camp or away.
- The hive holds at most **2** honey. A full hive waits and doesn't bank days.
- Cost: **4 wood and 6 fiber, no stone**. No prerequisite. Name "Beehive", description "Keep bees for honey all year".
- **Ground ruling (planning).** Every spot in the `north-outer` nook is under 2 tiles from the bakery, the honey tree or a wild tree. I checked this with a scan of the nook. So the hive takes the spec's fallback: ground `'north-far'`, standing at fire offset **(1.75, −7.25)**, which is tile (21.75, 7.75), east of the house. The `north-far` room list becomes `['house', 'beehive']`. The nook arcs are unchanged.
- Take refusals, in order and verbatim:
  1. 'Build the beehive first.'
  2. 'Return to camp to take the honey.'
  3. 'The bees have no honey for you yet.'
  4. 'Make room in your bag for the honey.'
- Hive honey isn't reachable for eating or cooking until taken. It never spoils.
- The wild honey tree is unchanged.
- Saves: `SAVE_VERSION` 27 → **28**. The upgrade step is idempotent, and the frozen v23 fixture is never edited.
- No journal line and no morning line (a ruling: the spec makes both optional, and a line every third dawn would be noise).
- Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. **A hive left full for many dawns, then emptied.** It makes exactly one honey at the next dawn, not one per missed cycle. Test in Task 2.
2. **A bag with one free space while the hive holds 2.** Take moves 1, leaves 1, and the message says 1. Test in Task 2.
3. **Hive honey is invisible to eating and cooking.** `availableFood(state, 'honey')` ignores it. Test in Task 2.
4. **A dawn spent away from camp** (in the quarry, or off cleared ground) still makes honey. Test in Task 2.
5. **A version-27 camp with the house built and the far ground cleared** loads with no hive and an empty one. Test in Task 1.

### Task 2: The bees make honey, and Take honey

**Files:**
- Create: `src/game/beehive.ts`
- Create: `tests/beehiveCamp.ts`, a helper `beehiveCamp({ honey?, nextHoney?, built?, day? })` modelled on `tests/bakehouseCamp.ts`. Clear `north-near` and `north-far`, set `structures.beehive` (default true), stand at the fire, and default to day 22 and `nextHoney` 25 when built.
- Modify: `src/game/rules.ts` (the `takeHoney` action in the `GameAction` union and `HANDLERS`; `build` sets `beehive`; `dawn()` calls `beehiveDawn`)
- Modify: `src/ui/App.ts` (only the `data-action` type union gains `'takeHoney'`)
- Test: `tests/beehive.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1's `GameState.beehive` and `BALANCE.hiveHoneyCapacity` / `hiveHoneyDays`.
- Produces:
  - `beehiveDawn(next: GameState): void`. It runs on the day just begun, mutating `next.beehive`. If the hive is built, `next.day >= nextHoney` and `honey < capacity`, then `honey += 1` and `nextHoney = next.day + hiveHoneyDays`. Otherwise it changes nothing, so a full hive's `nextHoney` stays in the past and it makes one honey at the first dawn after it's emptied.
  - `takeHoneyBlocker(state: GameState): string | null`, with the refusals listed in Global Constraints.
  - `takenHoney(state: GameState): { state: GameState; taken: number }`. It moves `min(honey, freeSpaces(state))` into `inventory.food.honey`.
  - Action `{ type: 'takeHoney' }`. It takes no time. Its message is `` `You take ${n} honey from the hive.` ``.
  - `build` with `structureId === 'beehive'` also sets `beehive: { honey: 0, nextHoney: state.day + BALANCE.hiveHoneyDays }`.
