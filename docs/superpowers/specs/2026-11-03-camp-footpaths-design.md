# Camp footpaths

## Intent
The camp footpaths should read as ground worn by walking. Their current broad, uniform brown fill and smooth outline look drawn over the meadow. The west route should meet the quarry track without a visible style break.

## Scope and constraints
Change the appearance of all camp footpaths, including the west path that opens with the shelter. Keep their routes, access, clearing entrance, tree clearance, winter snow behavior, and gameplay boundary. Keep the quarry floor and its broad track character.

## Approaches
- Chosen: use the quarry track's mottled pixels and irregular, soft edge on the camp routes. Keep the camp path near its current one-tile width, with a narrower worn centre and a light fringe. Taper the quarry track near its east exit so the boundary meets at the camp width.
- Considered: add texture within the existing camp path silhouettes. This keeps the simple edge that makes them look like marker strokes.

## Verification
Review fresh and built camp scenes, the open west route, and the quarry side of the crossing at day and in winter. Check that the paths stay connected, remain visually narrow, and do not grow into trees or rim stones. Run the focused map and art checks plus the build and lint checks.
