# Trail Food Design

At camp, a built drying rack turns one berry into one carried trail ration in 12 seconds. The conversion uses one bag slot before and after, so a full bag can still prepare a ration. A ration restores 45 hunger anywhere, compared with 25 for a raw berry. Fire cooking remains an immediate warm meal after dark and keeps its existing drying-rack bonus.

The bag shows berries and rations separately, includes both in capacity, and supports dropping either. A prepare button sits in a rack panel; an eat-ration button sits with survivor actions. The controls report why preparation is blocked. Saves move to version 10 and add zero rations to all older valid saves.

## Acceptance

- No berry is consumed without a rack, at camp, and at least one berry.
- Preparing a ration is a one-for-one conversion and never exceeds capacity.
- Eating consumes exactly one ration and restores 45 hunger, capped at 100.
- Older saves retain their progression and gain zero rations.
