# Path Discoveries Design

The three paths other than the lookout end at an old basket near (8, 8), a quiet spring near (10, 20), and a stone cairn near (31, 21). All are visible from day one. The player can interact within two tiles using V or the Explore nearby place button. The basket gives two berries once; the cairn gives two stone once. These finds require two free bag slots and are never lost to a full bag. The spring offers a repeatable twenty-second rest that restores 20 energy. The sidebar marks first visits without becoming a compulsory checklist.

Discovery flags persist in save version 9. Existing saves gain undiscovered sites without losing progress. Tests cover proximity, one-time rewards, bag capacity, repeatable spring use, save migration, and visual states.
